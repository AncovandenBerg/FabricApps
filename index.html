Fabric Apps

Small, self-contained Microsoft Fabric Apps built on European open data, with the source and the reasoning behind each one.

Heads up: Fabric Apps is in public preview. Breaking changes, shifting APIs and region gaps are expected. Nothing here is production code, and none of it runs on client data.

What is in here

AppWhat it doesData sourceStatus
windfingerprint Joins air quality measurements to wind direction so you can see where a station's pollution actually comes from. EEA air quality + Open-Meteo wind Live showcase
capacity-command A DP-700 operational simulator built with Rayfin on Fabric for the Fabric Apps community contest, Builder track. Simulated, no external source Live in Fabric
wake Explores maritime CO2 reporting under ETS and FuelEU, vessel by vessel. THETIS-MRV Planned
half-life Survival curves for vehicle registrations, the DAX-heavy one of the set. RDW open vehicle registry Planned
